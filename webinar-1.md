You are invited to the first webinar under the Caring for Knowledge project, focusing on the evolution of two community-led research guidelines.

#### The discussion will explore:

The Idu Mishmi Code of Research Ethics (IMCRE), developed by the Idu Mishmi Cultural and Literary Society (IMCLS).

Guidelines for Researchers Working in Southern Nicobar Islands, developed by the Tribal Council of Little Nicobar and Greater Nicobar.

#### Who should attend?

If your work intersects with the knowledge, heritage and lived experiences of Indigenous Peoples and Local Communities (IPLCs), this conversation is for you.

We particularly welcome community members, archivists, researchers, and practitioners working in museums, galleries and libraries.

#### About the discussion

This panel discussion offers a space to understand what led to the creation of these community guidelines, whom they serve, and how they shape and reshape the relationship between communities and those who engage with their knowledge.

More importantly, the conversation hopes to explore how we can support other communities in developing their own guidelines for caring for knowledge and the people.
